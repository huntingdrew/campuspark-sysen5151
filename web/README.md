# Driver interface

The Campus Driver side of the system. One screen is enough for the walking
skeleton: destination, arrival time and parking duration go in, a list of
eligible options comes back, the driver picks one and sees the confirmation.

Nothing is built here yet. It is the next piece of work after the stakeholder
requirements are written, because the fields on that form should come from the
requirements rather than from a guess about what looks good.

ORD requirements that land on this screen: 5.1.1.1 (session initiation),
5.1.1.3 (customer input), 5.1.2.1 (welcome screen), 5.1.2.3 (requests for
customer input), 5.1.2.5 (itinerary, here the parking options).
